MIDI Forge - installation
=========================

Copy the formats your DAW uses (most DAWs need just one), then rescan plug-ins in your DAW.

Windows (MIDI-Forge-Windows.zip)
--------------------------------
  VST3: copy "MIDI Forge.vst3" to  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
  CLAP: copy "MIDI Forge.clap" to  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\

macOS (MIDI-Forge-macOS.zip; Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  VST3:       copy "MIDI Forge.vst3"      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Audio Unit: copy "MIDI Forge.component" to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  CLAP:       copy "MIDI Forge.clap"      to  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/

  The plug-ins are not notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks them after a download. Unblock them once in
  the Terminal (leave out the formats you did not copy):

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"MIDI Forge.vst3"
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"MIDI Forge.component"
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/"MIDI Forge.clap"

  The macOS build is checked automatically but has not been tried in a DAW on a Mac yet.

Using it
--------
  Reaper: put MIDI Forge on a track's FX chain *before* the instrument.
  Bitwig: put MIDI Forge in the Note FX section of an instrument track (or before the instrument).
  Logic Pro: on a software instrument track, click the MIDI FX slot and choose
             Audio Units > ugerhard > MIDI Forge.
